## Script to record (~36s, ~95 words)

Same setup as before (car is fine), but:
- **Light:** face the window, not the window behind you.
- **Camera:** phone at eye level.
- **Framing:** keep your head in the middle third, because the top third holds the DM card.
- **Delivery:** speak naturally. Exact wording can change; I'll time the animation to whatever you actually say.

1. "If you're opening a med spa, here's one thing I'd set up before your first customer messages you."
2. "Someone DMs you: do you have anything Friday? They get an answer right away, with your open times and a link to book."
3. "If they pick a time, it's on your calendar. Done."
4. "Not everybody books, and that's fine. If they don't, your team gets a heads-up, and they get one friendly follow-up. Not ten."
5. "So that's the whole system. Message comes in, instant answer, they book, or a real person follows up."
6. "Would you have this set up before your med spa opens?"
